One Minute App Tour

What should I do first?

In your “Learning” or “Courses” tab, open the Mindfulness Challenge Series.

Start with Challenge #1: Awareness of Stress.

Every Challenge has a weekly follow-up.

Each week, a new challenge appears that builds on the prior Challenges.

How long is the Series?

Each Mindfulness Challenge Series is 12 weeks long.

What if I have technical issues or a question?

Email support@mindful12.com with a brief description, your device type, and a screenshot if possible.

What if I miss a week?

You won’t lose access. Open the most recent unlocked challenge and continue.

Can I replay past challenges?

Yes. All previously unlocked content remains available for review.

How long is each challenge?

Typically under 5 minutes.

The app isn’t loading. What should I do?

Fully close and reopen the app. Check your connection. If it continues, email support with your device model and a screenshot.

Is this a subscription?

Your first 12-week series is free; after that, you will have a payroll deduction of $1.00 per month.

How can I cancel my subscription?

In your app, you will find an “Unsubscribe” button.

How is Mindful12 different from meditation?

Meditation is a formal practice of focused attention.

Mindful12 trains you to bring that same awareness into real-time situations.

Meditation strengthens your ability to focus; Mindful12 helps you focus throughout your day.

What if I miss a challenge or reflection?

You can return to any past challenge at any time.

The series is flexible.

Do I need prior experience with mindfulness?

No. The program begins with simple awareness and breathing practices suitable for all levels.

Can mindfulness replace therapy or medication?

Mindful12 complements, but does not replace, professional medical or psychological care.

Will I feel results right away?

Some people feel calmer after one session; for others, it builds gradually.

Consistency matters more than speed — results come with practice.

How does mindfulness help at work?

It improves focus, patience, and emotional regulation.

Can I share the program with someone else?

Yes. Practicing with a partner or colleague increases accountability and enjoyment.

If your company sponsors Mindful12, each person should have their own login.

What happens after I finish the 12 weeks?

You can continue accessing your completed challenges, join new series, or explore the Mindfulness Library for ongoing learning.

Do I need special equipment?

No. Just your phone or computer and a few quiet moments to listen or read.

Are there scientific studies supporting mindfulness?

Yes. Research from Stanford, Harvard, and hundreds of medical research institutes shows mindfulness reduces stress hormones, improves focus, and enhances emotional regulation.

What should I do if I feel nothing is changing?

Keep practicing. Subtle shifts like breathing before reacting are progress.

Mindfulness develops gradually, not all at once.

How can I deepen my practice?

Revisit challenges, visit the Mindful 12 library, and take part in your company’s private community.

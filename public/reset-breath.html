The Reset Breath

A simple way to soften stress in real time

For one quiet moment, put the world on hold.
And let the words set the pace.
Read them slowly, and experience the Reset Breath.

Begin by noticing that you are breathing…

Now take a slow breath in through your nose…

Feel your chest gently expand.

Now slowly breathe out through your mouth…

Let your shoulders soften.
Feel your jaw relax.

Take one more breath

Slowly in through the nose…

Feel your chest gently expand.

And slowly out through the mouth…

Easy.
Quiet.
No force.

There…

Notice how you feel.

Your breath is always there to soften tension and ground you in the moment.

Come back to the Reset Breath as often as you like, letting the feeling grow more familiar and stay with you throughout your day.

Take the Reset Breath with you

A quiet minute whenever you need it.
